Short answer: Facebook's **Save video** option bookmarks the post inside Facebook. To get the file into your phone's gallery you need a browser download, then one extra step — Share → Save Video on iPhone, or a move into Movies/DCIM on Android.

This is the single most common misunderstanding about saving Facebook content, and it is not your fault. "Save" sounds like it means "download". Inside Facebook, it means "remember for later, inside the app".

## What Facebook's own buttons actually do

Facebook offers three lookalike actions, and none of them put an MP4 in your gallery:

- **Save** adds the post to your saved items list, visible only inside your account.
- **Share** opens the share sheet: Messenger, WhatsApp, email, or a link.
- **Download** on your own post exports the original file, but only for the person who uploaded it.

For a video someone else posted, the app gives you no file. That is by design, and it is why people look for a downloader in the first place.

## Step by step on iPhone

1. Open the public video, tap the share arrow, and choose **Copy link**.
2. Open Safari and load the [Facebook Video Downloader](/facebook-video-downloader).
3. Paste the link and tap the download button.
4. When the result appears, tap **Download**.
5. Open **Files → Downloads**, long-press the MP4, tap **Share**, then **Save Video**.
6. Check Photos → Recents.

If "Save Video" is absent, you are sharing an image thumbnail rather than the video file. Go back to Files and confirm the item plays with a video icon and a duration.

## Step by step on Android

1. Copy the video link from the Facebook app's share sheet.
2. Paste it into the downloader in Chrome.
3. Tap **Download**. Chrome saves to **Downloads** and shows a notification.
4. Open **Files by Google** (or your file manager) → Downloads.
5. Long-press the MP4 → **Move to** → **Movies** (or **DCIM/Camera**).
6. Your gallery app will index it, usually within a minute.

Android galleries only scan specific folders. A file sitting in Downloads may be invisible in Photos even though it exists. Moving it to Movies or DCIM is the fix, not a re-download.

## Which Facebook links work

Our resolver handles public **facebook.com** posts, **fb.watch** links, Reels, and Watch pages. Copy the link from the post itself, not from a Messenger preview or a screenshot of a link.

What does not work:

- **Private groups and friends-only posts.** The resolver visits the page the way a logged-out visitor would. If you would see a login wall, so does it.
- **Deleted or removed videos.** Nothing can recover a file the platform no longer serves.
- **Live streams that already ended without a saved replay.** If Facebook did not keep it, there is nothing to resolve.
- **Videos you can only see because you are logged in.** Your session never leaves your device; we do not use it.

That last point matters for trust: no cookies, no password, no account. It also explains the boundary. Public content resolves; content that requires your identity does not.

## Sound, quality and format

Facebook delivers MP4, which both iOS and Android play natively. Two things are worth knowing:

**Split streams.** Some videos arrive as a video-only track plus a separate audio track. The player pairs them so you hear sound while previewing, and the download keeps the pairing. If a clip plays silently in your gallery, the source genuinely had no audio track — a silent autoplay video, for example.

**Resolution ceilings.** You get the best rendition Facebook serves publicly. HD is common, 4K is rare, and some older uploads are SD only. A downloader cannot exceed the source.

Also expect a Facebook watermark or overlay if the original video had one. Removing branding is not something we do, and editing it out usually breaks the terms you agreed to.

## FAQ

**Does Facebook notify someone when I download their video?**
No. There is no download notification, and no account is involved in the request.

**Why does my saved Facebook video have no sound?**
Either the original was silent, or the file was saved from a preview that only included video. Re-run the download and use the Download button on the result, not a long-press on the player.

**Can I download a video from a private Facebook group?**
No. Private content requires your login, and we never ask for it.

**Where do Facebook downloads go on Android?**
Chrome's Downloads folder. Move the file to Movies or DCIM so your gallery indexes it.

**Do fb.watch links work?**
Yes. They redirect to the same public video, and the resolver follows the redirect.

**Does the download include captions or subtitles?**
Burned-in captions travel with the video. Separate caption tracks are not attached to the MP4.
